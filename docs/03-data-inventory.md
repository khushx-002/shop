## Customers, orders, coupons

These are NOT public and could not be read without WordPress admin or database access:

- Customer accounts (wp_users with role customer, billing/shipping meta).
- Order history (wc_orders / shop_order posts, order items, Razorpay payment ids).
- Coupons: only two codes are known. JIVO200 (advertised on the home page as flat 200 off above 5000) and JIVO100 both exist on the live site but currently respond "This coupon has expired".
- Shipping zones: observed from the Store API - Delhi NCR pincodes (110xxx, 122xxx, 201xxx) get a flat rate of 100 and free shipping above 999; every other state returned NO shipping method (checkout not possible). The home page promises free delivery above 499 all over India, so this looks like a misconfiguration that the business must confirm.
- Taxes: none configured (no tax lines on any cart total).
- Payment: Razorpay only (cards, UPI, net banking, EMI through Razorpay checkout).
